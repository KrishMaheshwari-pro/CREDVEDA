Did the score actually predict repayment?

Loans grouped by the probability the model gave them at funding time, against what those borrowers actually did

This is the model's report card measured on money actually lent, not a held-out test split. Repayment behaviour is driven by each borrower's true creditworthiness, which the model never sees — so if the green bars climb left to right, the score is carrying real information.

The two bars measure different things and are not expected to match in level: blue is the predicted probability of a good overall outcome, green is the share of individual instalments paid on time. A borrower can end up a poor outcome while still paying most instalments, which is why green sits higher. What matters here is that they move together — that is discrimination. Calibration is tested separately on the model report card.
